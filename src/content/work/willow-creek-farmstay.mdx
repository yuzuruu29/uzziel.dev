Willow Creek Farmstay is a fictional portfolio sample for a rural accommodation host. The page shows how a farm stay can replace scattered Airbnb, Instagram, Facebook, and email inquiries with one focused inquiry flow.

The sample includes a sticky header, hero CTA, quick facts bar, stay details, farm experiences, rates, gallery, location section, house rules, FAQ accordion, inquiry CTA, full inquiry form, and a thank-you state.

The technical goal was to keep the build static and portable: semantic HTML, mobile-first CSS, labeled form fields, vanilla JavaScript for the FAQ and submit state, and no framework dependency.
