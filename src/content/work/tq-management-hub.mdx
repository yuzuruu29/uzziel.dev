The Techquarters Management Hub turns an agency ledger into a working decision surface. Dashboard KPIs cover net profit, recurring revenue, runway, and anomalies, with drill-down into the transactions behind each number.

The MVP includes stackable ledger filters, department profit-and-loss rollups, category rules, staged CSV mapping and approval, confidence-marked categorization, and a monthly narrative generated from computed aggregates.

The Next.js build uses TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Recharts, PapaParse, Jest, and React Testing Library. A seeded in-memory client mirrors the Supabase query shape, while migrations and row-level-security templates document the production handoff.
