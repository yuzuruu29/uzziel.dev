Ancestra is the opposite of a prompt-engineering app. You pick an old family photo, tap Restore, compare before/after, export the result. No sliders, no inpainting UI, no tutorial video.

The stack splits cleanly: an Expo React Native app in `mobile/` and a FastAPI backend in `api/`. Phase 8 shipped QA and launch-readiness polish. June 2026 work wired the real provider — Replicate with CodeFormer — plus multipart upload, background restore jobs with explicit phase tracking, local image caching so restored URLs do not expire on the phone, and HEIC/HEIF labeling for iOS uploads.

I started it after scanning lola's albums and realizing most restoration tools assume you already know what "denoise strength" means. The product bet is that Filipino families have boxes of faded prints and zero patience for diffusion model jargon.

Still in flight: store listing, pricing, and enough restore-quality QA on real bayan photos to trust a public launch.
