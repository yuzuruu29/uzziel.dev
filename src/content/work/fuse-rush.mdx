Fuse Rush is an original one-minute mobile game built around quick pattern recognition. Tap two matching orthogonal tiles to fuse them into the next value, refill the board, and keep the chain alive before the 1.8-second multiplier window closes.

Every fifth fusion grants a boost: more time, a low-value board refresh, or a temporary score multiplier. High scores stay on the device.

The build uses Expo, React Native, TypeScript, haptics, linear color surfaces, and AsyncStorage. The project includes pure game-logic tests, TypeScript validation, and Android and iOS export commands.
