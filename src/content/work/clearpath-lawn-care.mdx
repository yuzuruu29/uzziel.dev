ClearPath Lawn Care is a fictional portfolio sample for a local home service business. The page is built around one job: collect enough information for a clear written quote before the owner replies.

The sample includes service cards, service area copy, a three-step process, pricing guidance, quote factors, before-and-after placeholders, trust signals, FAQ accordion, quote CTA, full quote form, and a thank-you state.

The design keeps the conversion path simple on mobile. The form collects address, service type, frequency, yard size, schedule notes, and extra context without requiring a backend.
