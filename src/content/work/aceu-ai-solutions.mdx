ACEU AI Solutions is the two-founder AI engineering studio I run with Aaron Christian Panergayo. The portfolio site had one brief: look like a studio that ships cinematic work, without a framework, a build step, or a CMS bill. The whole site is one `index.html` on Netlify, with Lenis and GSAP from CDN.

The brand is void black, charcoal, and one signature blue, set in Space Grotesk with JetBrains Mono labels. A live SVG orbital logo spins in the corner. Behind the content sit the cybernetic layers: a GLSL shader backdrop, a particle constellation, grid, grain, soft scanlines, and a glow cursor that switches to a VIEW state over project cards. First load runs a cinematic boot sequence into the hero.

## Motion as the product demo

For an AI studio, the site itself is the first portfolio piece, so the motion got engineering attention. Buttons are GSAP-owned magnetics with a label roll, elastic release, and a tactile press. The capability marquee reads scroll velocity and speeds up with it. Cards wear HUD corner brackets; the hero has pointer-tracked depth; scroll choreography runs on ScrollTrigger with Flip for state changes.

## Built to be edited by non-designers

Everything a founder needs to update lives in five JavaScript arrays at the top of the file — `PROJECTS`, `FOUNDERS`, `SERVICES`, `EMAIL`, and the contact form config. Adding a case study means appending an object, not touching markup. The work section currently showcases ApplyGuard PH, Needs vs. Wants, and Pledgr, each pulling from the studio's shipped products.

Contact runs through Netlify Forms with an AJAX submit that notifies the studio inbox. No backend, no secrets, nothing to maintain — which is the correct amount of infrastructure for a site whose job is to get the first reply.
