Filipino remote job seekers paste the same sketchy Upwork post into three group chats asking "legit ba 'to?" ApplyGuard PH is the tool I wished existed — paste a post, add a few quick details, get a verdict (Apply / Caution / Skip), a fit score out of 100, a scam-risk level, missing-info flags, and a copy-paste prompt for your own ChatGPT or Claude thread.

Everything runs client-side. No sign-up, no subscription, no paid APIs. Persistence is localStorage only. The May 2026 MVP added premium motion on the paste box and scan-form inputs after the core scanner shipped.

I built it because the micro-agency and Yusel AI work taught me how Filipinos actually evaluate trust online — fast, skeptical, and usually on mobile. ApplyGuard is the free public slice of that instinct: help someone decide before they waste an afternoon on a bad application.
