The Process

How to Preorder

1

Submit your inquiry with order details

Tell us what you’d like to order, your preferred pickup date, and how many servings you need. The more detail you include, the faster we can confirm.

2

We confirm and send your total

We’ll review your request and reply within 24 hours with availability confirmation and a written total. No surprises.

3

Pay and pick up on your chosen date

Once confirmed, pay via your preferred method and collect your order fresh on the agreed date. Delivery available within our service area.

Getting Your Order

Pickup & Delivery

Pickup

Pickup is available Tuesday to Saturday between 10 AM and 5 PM from our confirmed kitchen pickup point. Pickup is always free.

You’ll receive a confirmation message with your exact pickup window once your order is confirmed.

Delivery

Local delivery is available within a 15 km radius for orders over $120. Enter your address in the inquiry form and we’ll confirm delivery availability.

Delivery times and fees are confirmed when we reply to your inquiry.

🕐

Lead Times & Cut-offs

Weekly preorder cut-off: Friday by 5 PM. Catering and party tray orders require a minimum of 5–7 days’ advance notice to allow time for preparation. Same-day orders are not available.

🌿

Dietary Information

We accommodate common dietary needs including gluten-free, dairy-free, vegetarian, and vegan options on request. Note all allergies and dietary requirements in your inquiry form so we can confirm whether we can accommodate your needs before accepting your order.
