## 3. Best For cards

### Couples

A simple place to rest, cook, read, and spend time without a packed schedule. Good for couples who want quiet mornings, open space, and evenings by the fire.

### Families

Children can see the animals, walk the garden, and learn where some of their food comes from. The cabin works best for families who are comfortable with a real farm setting, not a hotel setup.

### Remote Workers

The cabin gives you a quiet place to focus between walks, coffee breaks, and fresh air. This is best for remote workers who want a slower work week and do not need a busy town nearby.

### Small Groups

A good fit for close friends or small family groups who want a private rural stay. The space is meant for simple meals, conversation, farm walks, and relaxed evenings.

## 4. Farm Experiences cards

### Animal visits

Guests can visit the animals with a host during scheduled times. We keep visits calm and supervised so the animals stay safe and guests know what to expect.

### Garden walks

Walk through the garden and see what is growing during your stay. The garden changes by season, so some visits are full and others are quieter.

### Campfire evenings

Spend the evening outside with a small campfire when weather and local conditions allow. We provide the fire area, and guests bring their own snacks, drinks, and stories.

### Seasonal harvest

When something is ready to pick, guests may be invited to join a simple harvest activity. This depends on the season, weather, and what the farm is growing at the time.

## 8. FAQ answers

### Is Willow Creek Farmstay a real working farm?

Yes, the concept is based on a small working farm stay experience. Guests should expect normal farm sounds, outdoor paths, animals, changing weather, and a setting that feels lived-in rather than polished.

### What is included in the stay?

The stay includes use of the private cabin, basic amenities, outdoor seating, and scheduled farm experiences when available. Some activities depend on weather, season, animal care schedules, and host availability.

### How many guests can stay?

The cabin is best for couples, small families, remote workers, or small groups. The final guest limit should be confirmed through the inquiry form before booking.

### Are pets allowed?

Pets are not automatically accepted because this is a farm with animals on the property. If guests want to bring a pet, they should include details in the inquiry so the host can review it first.

### Is there Wi-Fi?

The cabin is intended to support quiet stays and light remote work. Guests should ask about current Wi-Fi details before booking, especially if they need it for calls, uploads, or full workdays.

### Can guests join farm activities?

Yes, guests can join simple farm activities when they are available and safe. Activities may change based on the season, weather, animal needs, and the daily work on the farm.

### How does booking work?

Guests send an inquiry with their dates, guest count, and stay details. The host reviews availability, answers questions, and sends the next steps if the stay is a good fit.

### What should guests bring?

Guests should bring personal items, comfortable outdoor shoes, weather-appropriate clothes, groceries or snacks, and anything needed for children or work. The farm is quiet, so it is better to bring what you need before arriving.
